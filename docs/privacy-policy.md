# Privacy Policy for Attendance Flow

**Last updated:** July 26, 2026

SuperJump Dev ("we", "us", or "our") operates the **Attendance Flow** mobile application (the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App. We designed Attendance Flow to work **entirely on your device** and to **not collect personal data on our servers**.

---

## Summary

- No account or sign-in is required
- Attendance data is stored **locally on your device only**
- We do **not** sell your data
- We do **not** use third-party analytics or advertising in the App
- The App does **not** require internet access to function

---

## Information the App Stores

When you use Attendance Flow, the App stores the following information **on your device**:

- Dates you mark as present on the calendar
- Your selected start month (if set)
- App preferences needed to restore your attendance history

This data is saved using your device's local storage (SharedPreferences / on-device storage). It is **not transmitted to us** or to any server we operate.

---

## Information We Do Not Collect

Attendance Flow does **not** collect, store, or process on our systems:

- Name, email address, or phone number
- Location data
- Contacts or photos
- Device identifiers for tracking
- Usage analytics
- Payment or financial information

Because we do not receive your data, we also do not create user profiles or share personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

---

## Internet and Third-Party Services

The App is designed to work **offline**. It does not require an internet connection for core features.

We do not integrate third-party analytics, advertising networks, or social login providers in the App.

If you download the App from **Google Play**, Google may collect information according to its own policies when you use the store or when the device communicates with Google services (for example, for app updates or Play Protect). That processing is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## Data Retention and Deletion

Your attendance data remains on your device until you:

- Clear the App's data in your device settings, or
- Uninstall the App

Uninstalling the App or clearing its storage permanently removes locally stored attendance data from your device. We cannot recover this data because we never receive it.

---

## Children's Privacy

Attendance Flow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will assist where possible (note: since data stays on-device, deletion is typically done by uninstalling the App or clearing app data on the device).

---

## Security

We rely on your device's operating system to protect locally stored data. Because information does not leave your device through the App, there is no separate cloud account to secure on our side.

We recommend keeping your device locked and updated with the latest security patches.

---

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding personal data (such as access, correction, or deletion). Since Attendance Flow stores data only on your device and we do not receive it, you can exercise these rights directly by managing or deleting the App's data on your device.

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or other regions with data protection laws, you may contact us with questions about this policy.

---

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.

For material changes, we may also update the policy link in the Google Play listing.
